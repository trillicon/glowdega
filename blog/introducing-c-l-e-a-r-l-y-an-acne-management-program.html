There is no reason for Acne to stop your shine. Yet, every day, we find ourselves covering up our blemishes, being frustrated over new breakouts, and constantly on the hunt for a holy grail product that will finally end the acne once and for all. It is reported that people with acne spend an average of $200 per month between in-office treatments and over-the-counter products. That's more than $2,400 a year for a face that never seems to get clear. Well, it's about to be a new year and we're off that. We're only getting into acne treatments and products that are proven effective and work to get over 90% of clients clear.

Meet C L E A R L Y

Acne does not have to be frustrating, confusing, or any reason to feel less confident in yourself. If clear and smooth skin is what you desire, that’s exactly what you will get with C L E A R L Y by FAIRY GLOW MOTHER®, a Face Reality powered skin care treatment program. Success in C L E A R L Y requires commitment to bi-weekly treatments and being diligent with your home care regimen. Unlike other acne treatments available, this program provides you with research and education on your specific acne type(s) along with proven acne treatments and award-winning products together at an affordable price. Once you complete your consultation, you will have the option to enroll in a 3-month, 6-month, or 9-month program.

C L E A R L Y + YOU

This program is best suited for those with mild to severe inflamed acne and those who have had persistent breakouts for at least six consecutive months. For this reason, you are required to book a consultation before you can gain access to enroll.

C L E A R L Y is for acne on the face, neck, and back.

How long does this program take?

There are many factors that affect the total duration of the C L E A R L Y process such as type of acne, your skin type, and skin sensitivity amongst other things. It is also important to note that it can take up to 90 days for acne to appear on the surface of the skin.

As  everyone is unique, the C L E A R L Y process can take anywhere from 3-6 months. In more severe cases, it can take a little longer. You will be given an estimate of how long you will need to be in the program following your consultation.

The Clear Commitment

As a C L E A R L Y client, you will be agreeing to do your custom home care regimen twice daily, bi-weekly skin care treatments at FAIRY GLOW MOTHER®, and making any recommending lifestyle changes that may negatively impact your desired skin care results for a minimum of three months.  This is an exclusive program meaning you can’t use any outside products while going through the C L E A R L Y process. 

As your Certified Acne Specialist, I will be checking in with you frequently and performing analysis to ensure we are achieving your skin care goals in the fastest way possible. Occasionally this may mean that I advise we switch your products up which may or may not require you to purchase new products. 

How can I join?

If you're interested in joining C L E A R L Y at any level, you must book a consultation. To do so, please select the C L E A R L Y Acne Consultation add-on when booking an Essential Glow Customized Facial. A link to enroll and payment options will be provided only after your consultation.
