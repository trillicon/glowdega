This episode explores building a strong beauty industry brand using the "C.U.N.T." framework.

Want to make your mark on the beauty industry and stand out amongst over 50,000 estheticians? This episode dives into what makes iconic beauty businesses thrive, from clever naming strategies to finding your perfect niche. Learn why 72% of top-performing brands focus on customer experience, and how repeat clients spend 67% more than new ones. Whether you're dreaming of launching a specialized brow studio or a results-driven skincare clinic, discover proven strategies to build a sustainable beauty empire that transcends trends.
