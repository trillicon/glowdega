A hot, new bombshell is entering GLOWDEGA!

Starting in September, Sabrina will be taking clients on select Sundays. Sabrina is an acne expert and the owner of Threo Skin which had been based in Pasadena, CA. (Whenever I was working in SoCal, I would be working out of her space.) She has been lauded by several professional brands for her ability to not only get clients clear but to correct and repair long-term damage caused by acne. And she does it all without any prescriptions or invasive treatments!

A few weeks ago Sabrina made the difficult decision to close her Pasadena studio so she could focus a bit more on family. You see, Sabrina is a military wife and though her family had been stationed near Pasadena when she started, they actually had to relocate up to NorCal two years ago. So while she isn’t committing to full-time just yet, you can see Sabrina every other Sunday. She will be focusing on corrective care and peels and her books are open NOW!

Speaking of peels… chemical peel season is quickly approaching. This year, ALL clients seeking peel services must reserve their peels by September 15, 2025. The reservation fee goes towards your full-sized, custom after-care kits. Click the button below to reserve now. And if you aren’t sure if you need a peel or which peel is right for you, come on out on Friday, September 5th for our Peel Plan Party (it’s free)!
