Introduction

What Information We Collect

We collect several types of information about you from various sources:

  • Contact details: Your name, address, phone number, and email address.

  • Order information: Billing address, shipping address, payment details, and phone number.

  • Customer support information: Any details you include in communications with us.

Information About Your Usage

We automatically collect certain information about how you access and use our Site and Services, including:

  • Device information (e.g., device type, operating system)

  • Browser information

  • Information about your interaction with our Services

Information from Third Parties

We may obtain information about you from third parties, including:

  • Service providers like Squarespace

  • Payment processors

  • Marketing partners

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

Providing Products and Services

  • Process payments and fulfill orders

  • Create and manage your account

  • Arrange shipping and handle returns/exchanges

  • Send notifications about your transactions

Marketing and Advertising

  • Send promotional communications (with your consent where required)

  • Show you targeted advertisements

  • Improve our marketing efforts

Security and Fraud Prevention

  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or illegal activities

  • Protect the security of our Services and users

Communicating with You and Improving Our Services

  • Provide customer support

  • Analyze and improve our Services

Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve our Site and Services. These technologies help us understand user behavior, remember your preferences, and provide personalized experiences.

You can control cookie settings through your browser, but please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our Site.

We do not sell your personal information in the traditional sense. However, our use of certain advertising and analytics cookies may constitute a "sale" or "sharing" under some state laws. You can control these cookies as described in the "Your Rights" section.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have some or all of the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

  • Deletion: Ask us to delete your personal information from our records.

  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate information about you.

  • Restrict processing: In certain circumstances, request that we limit how we use your data.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information in the "Contact Us" section. We will respond to your request within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect or "sell" (as defined by applicable law) personal information from children without parental consent.

Security and Data Retention

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure. We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

International Data Transfers

We may transfer and process your information in countries other than your own. We use appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for international data transfers as required by applicable laws.
