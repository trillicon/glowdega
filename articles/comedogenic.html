WTF Does Comedogenic Mean?

The comedogenic scale is a tempting shortcut for acne-prone skin, but finished formulations matter far more than a single ingredient rating. Concentration, vehicle, rinse-off versus leave-on use, formulation interactions, and individual skin differences all change how a product behaves.
