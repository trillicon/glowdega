ROSACEA ROLL CALL

2024-04-03

April is Rosacea Awareness Month and though it is a chronic skin disease that affects nearly 10% of the world's population, it doesn't get the same PR treatment that other chronic skin conditions do. This post breaks down all you need to know about Rosacea and includes some helpful tips on how to manage the condition. 

Rosacea is a chronic, inflammatory skin condition primarily affecting the face, characterized by episodes of flushing, persistent redness, visible blood vessels, swollen red bumps, and sometimes eye problems such as dryness and irritation. Its exact cause is unknown, but it may involve a combination of hereditary and environmental factors. It is often triggered by factors that increase blood flow to the surface of the skin, such as hot foods or beverages, spicy foods, alcohol, temperature extremes, sunlight or wind, emotions, exercise, cosmetics, and certain medications or medical conditions. This skin condition can mimic others like acne or eczema, making diagnosis challenging. Dermatologists typically diagnose the condition based on the history of symptoms and a physical examination of the skin.

The best skin care products for managing rosacea will include azelaic acid, tranexamic acid, hydrating properties, and SPF. A skin care routine for someone with rosacea tends to be more on the gentle and hydrating side with limited actives.
